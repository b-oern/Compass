More than a compass: Compass Path records your route as a polyline – no GPS required.

The direction comes from the compass, the distance from the step counter. Every time you add a point, the segment you walked is calculated from your heading and the counted steps (times your step length) and appended to the path. Step by step, a sketch of your route takes shape.

Path recording:
* Polyline built from compass heading and step counter
* Adjustable step length (0.1 – 3 m)
* Altitude via barometer, segments colored by elevation
* Optional: record GPS alongside and compare its deviation from the step path
* Continue recording from any point of the current day
* Day view and list view of all entries
* Export as CSV or JSON

Compass:
* Points to magnetic north or true north
* Exact degrees and cardinal direction
* Displays sensor status
* Haptic feedback
* Light and dark theme

No advertisements. No trackers.
